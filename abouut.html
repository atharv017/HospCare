About US

Founded with a vision of delivering accessible, compassionate, and quality healthcare, our hospital has grown into a trusted multi-speciality healthcare centre serving patients with dedication and excellence. Over the years, we have expanded our medical services, infrastructure, and team of experienced healthcare professionals to provide comprehensive treatment under one roof. Our hospital brings together specialist doctors, skilled nurses, trained paramedical staff, and modern medical technology to ensure that every patient receives personalised and reliable care. We are committed to maintaining high standards of patient safety, clinical excellence, and continuous quality improvement. From routine consultations and diagnostic services to advanced treatments and critical care, we offer a wide range of healthcare facilities designed to meet the diverse needs of our community.

Vision And Mission

Our Vision

We strive to grow relentlessly, challenge conventions, and lead with a pioneering spirit while transforming patient care.

Our Mission

To be a healthcare destination that provides compassionate, personalised care to each patient, by combining the latest technology and medical expertise.

Our Management

Mr. Urvaksh Bhote

Chief Operating Officer

Mr. Urvaksh brings over 26 years of extensive expertise in global business operations, finance & accounting, change management, transformation, and operational excellence to our team. Before joining us, he served as the CEO and Board Member at DecisivEdge Technology Services India Pvt. Ltd. Mr. Urvaksh holds a bachelors in commerce and a masters in business administration, specialising in finance. He is a certified project management professional from PMI, Certified Lean Six Sigma Green and Black Belt, and holds a diploma in import and export management and materials management.

Dr. Purvez K. Grant

Chairman & Managing Trustee

The Chief Cardiologist and Chairman of Cardiovascular Services at the hospital, Dr. Purvez K. Grant, is the pillar of the legacy of Ruby Hall Clinic. One of the most sought-after specialists in cardiology, Dr. Grant holds a slew of fellowships from some of the most prestigious establishments in the world, including the American College of Cardiology and the Royal Society of Medicine, UK, among others. Also, in recognition of his services to the medical field, Dr. Purvez K. Grant has been conferred several honours, including three national awards.
